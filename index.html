How we work

A coach, not another meditation app

Apps are great at reminders and terrible at everything else. They don't know that you've skipped nine days, that sitting still makes you anxious, or that your evenings fall apart at 9 p.m.

At Veyloora you get a person. They build a practice around your actual week, read your check-ins, and change the plan when life gets in the way — which it will.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

I can't stop thinking. Am I doing it wrong?

No. Noticing that your mind wandered is the practice, not a sign of failure. Everyone's mind wanders, including your coach's.

How long until I notice something?

Most people notice small changes within three to four weeks of near-daily practice. We don't promise specific outcomes, and anyone who does is overselling.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching supports everyday stress and habit building. It is not therapy or medical care, and it doesn't replace a licensed professional. If something bigger is going on, we'll say so and suggest you get proper support.

Do I need to sit cross-legged on the floor?

No. A chair is fine. Several programs include walking and lying-down practices.

What if I miss a week?

Nothing happens. You tell your coach, and the plan restarts where you are rather than where you were supposed to be.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Ready to try ten quiet minutes?

Twenty minutes on a call to hear what you're dealing with and suggest a starting point. Free, no pressure.
